Accuracy and efficiency

Accuracy is the share of cards in the correct category. Efficiency is the number of cards placed divided by the number of moves. Moving a card again adds a move. Two players can make the same number of moves and get different numbers of cards right.

Score combines correctness with how you got there. A correctly placed card earns two points on its first move, one on its second, and zero after that.

After fixes12 of 121675%
