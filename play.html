    Rules

    Pick one or more items in the tray, then tap a category box to put them there. Each item belongs in exactly one category.

    To move an item again, select it and tap a different category, or tap the tray to take it back. If nothing is selected, tapping a category heading selects every item in that category.

    Scoring: an item in the right category scores 2 points if you got it there on your first move, 1 point on your second move, and 0 after that.

    Efficiency is the number of items currently placed in categories divided by your total moves. Each item you move counts as its own move.
